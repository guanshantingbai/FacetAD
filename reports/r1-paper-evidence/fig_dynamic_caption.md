**Image-level ranking under a frozen Dynamic correction.** Each row pairs an anomalous and a normal query from the same class. The gap is the anomalous probability minus the normal probability after averaging three members. The recipient image and static A/H midpoint are fixed for all four columns. The donor changes only the abnormal-prototype displacement. Positive gaps give the correct ordering. Dynamic leaves the deployed heatmap unchanged. Relative IDs, labels and per-image logit changes are in fig_dynamic_pairs.csv. The three examples are diagnostic selections.
