**Local residual evidence in real test queries.** White pixels denote ground-truth defects. All panels share the same registered coordinates and the heatmaps use one absolute [0,1] color scale. H residual off retains the trained native calibration in H alone. A/H fusion averages the two plain full-resolution maps. Final R1 also uses color-guided interpolation. Changes under each row are within-image foreground/background AUROC differences. For native miss, partial repair, boundary and damage the displayed differences are fusion minus A, fusion minus H, final minus fusion (14-pixel boundary band), and H minus A, respectively. Examples were selected by the predeclared ranks, not by heatmap appearance. The last candidate page shows the remaining three below-chance A examples that failed the recovery criterion. The displays do not estimate event prevalence outside this query set.
